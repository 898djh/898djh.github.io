About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer and Information Science at the University of Michigan–Dearborn. I previously received a double master’s degree in Computer Science from the CCNU–UOW Joint Institute. My current research interests lie in distributed computing, with a focus on building systems that help software developers create performance-optimal applications.
