About Me

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Computing & Software Systems at the University of Washington Bothell . My research lies broadly in computer systems, with a particular interest in building efficient systems to support modern applications, including AI and networked applications.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer and Information Science from the University of Michigan–Dearborn . During my Ph.D., I also had the opportunity to work with Cisco Research .
